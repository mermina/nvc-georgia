Team

Bětka Wójcik

Certified CNVC trainer · Facilitator

Certified CNVC trainer and facilitator with over 10 years of experience, passionate about designing learning journeys and hosting brave spaces for dialogue.

Her dream of a world where everyone’s needs matter inspires and motivates her work with individuals and communities across different countries. She fell in love with Georgia even before visiting it for the first time in 2008. Since then, she has kept returning — guided by her love for the people and the land that feels like home.

Nino Margvelashvili

Relational learning and well-being steward

Nino is a certified Self-Reg® Facilitator and ICDP Trainer, with further training in Nonviolent Communication and mindfulness practices.

Since 2009 she has worked with children, young people, parents and educators across schools and communities, supporting self-regulation, emotional wellbeing, inclusive learning and healthy relationships. Much of her work has focused on helping adults understand children’s behaviour through the lens of stress, development and connection, facilitating relational processes that strengthen the conditions for wellbeing and flourishing. Nino is an author of a children's book “Now You Can Relax, Let Go, and Rest.”

Jana Kowalová

Community social worker · Youth worker · Facilitator

Community social worker, youth worker, and facilitator passionate about bringing people together and supporting communities in discovering their own strengths.

She believes in the power of connection and collaboration, and enjoys creating space where people feel heard and encouraged to take initiative and shape positive change in their surroundings. She is the co-founder of Kona, a community-based organisation running a youth and community centre in the village of Kvemo Chala, Georgia. Nonviolent Communication helps her stay curious, connect with people and their needs, and build trust and understanding across differences.

Nino Lotishvili

Intercultural communication specialist · Peacebuilding practitioner · UNDP Insider Mediator

Nino Lotishvili is an intercultural communication specialist, peacebuilding practitioner, and UNDP Insider Mediator.

Her work focuses on intercultural dialogue, Nonviolent Communication (NVC), emotional resilience, and trauma-informed peacebuilding, emphasizing personal responsibility and inner transformation. Nino is the founder of ManaTheia Peace Hub, an initiative integrating emotional resilience into peacebuilding across Georgia and the wider Caucasus region. She supports individuals and communities in reconnecting with their inner wisdom and building compassionate, inclusive, and peaceful societies.
